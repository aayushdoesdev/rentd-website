Questions owners ask.

Does my property stay in my name?

Always. RENTD never takes your property on lease or sublets it. You remain the owner and the landlord on the agreement — we act on your behalf and on your instructions.

What if the tenant stops paying?

Your manager escalates on a fixed schedule — reminder, call, formal notice — and you see every step. We do not guarantee or advance rent, and we'd rather say that here than have you find out later.

Who pays for repairs?

You do. We get the quote, manage the vendor and close the job. Anything above the limit you set waits for your yes.

Can I still choose the tenant?

Yes. We shortlist and verify, you approve. Nothing is signed without you.

I'm abroad. How does this work for me?

Better than for anyone else. You approve repairs from your phone, get dated photo inspections twice a year, and every agreement, receipt and TDS record is filed as it happens.

What does ₹1,000 a year actually include?

The software: agreement, reminders, UPI collection, receipts, documents, condition reports, renewal alerts and your annual statement. No humans, no site visits — that's what keeps it ₹83 a month.

Is ₹1,000 per property or for all my properties?

Per property. An owner with five flats pays ₹5,000 a year, and each one gets its own agreement, ledger and vault.
